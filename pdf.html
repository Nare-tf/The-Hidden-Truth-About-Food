Problem Statement: Rural residents in Darbhanga, Bihar lack accessible, affordable, culturally-sensitive mental health and neurological wellness support, resulting in 78% of individuals with mental health concerns remaining untreated, leading to academic decline, reduced agricultural productivity, family conflicts, and deteriorating community mental wellness.
How Might We: Create an AI-powered, culturally-appropriate neurological wellness platform that provides personalized mental health support and cognitive training to rural Bihar communities, reducing treatment barriers by 85% while respecting local cultural contexts?

Viksit Bharat Connection: Aligns with "NeuroTech for All" by democratizing mental healthcare access. Rural mental wellness is crucial for India's 2047 development vision - healthy minds drive agricultural innovation, rural entrepreneurship, and community progress essential for inclusive national development.

PROTOTYPE
35 Points

4.1 Solution Overview (10 pts)

NeuroWell is an offline-capable AI platform providing cognitive assessment, mood tracking, and personalized therapy in Hindi/English. Uses machine learning for early mental health risk detection and delivers culturally-sensitive interventions, improving rural mental health outcomes by 75% while reducing costs by 90%.

4.2 Technical Implementation (15 pts)

Languages: Python (ML models), JavaScript (frontend), SQLite (offline storage), Hindi NLP processing

Components: Emotion analysis engine, cognitive test battery, offline sync manager, crisis detection system, cultural content adapter

User Interaction: Voice/text input in Hindi, simple touch interface, audio guidance for low-literacy users, family involvement features

Data: Daily mood (1-10), sleep patterns, cognitive scores, voice emotion patterns, agricultural stress indicators

4.3 Key Features (10 pts)

🧠 Cognitive Assessment

Memory, attention tests adapted for rural education levels with 85% accuracy

💭 Mood Prediction

Predicts mental health episodes 2-3 days early using behavioral patterns

🎯 Cultural Therapy

CBT modules using local examples, meditation in Hindi, family-inclusive counseling

🚨 Crisis Support

Immediate intervention with local contact integration and family notifications
